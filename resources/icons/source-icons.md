# Bundled Icons

Service logos identify [Navidrome (GPL-3.0)](https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome) and [OpenSubsonic (Apache-2.0)](https://github.com/opensubsonic/open-subsonic-api).

External service logos come from [Last.fm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Last.fm_favicon.png) and [MusicBrainz](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MusicBrainz_Logo_Icon_(2016).svg).

Logos may be resized or adapted for display; their inclusion does not imply affiliation.

Most icons either come directly from the [GNOME Icon Development Kit (CC0-1.0)](https://gitlab.gnome.org/Teams/Design/icon-development-kit/-/tree/156946fbae49beff62bd6ac1503f11c5cc6dc548/icons), or are modified slightly.
